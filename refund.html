Your refund checklist

A clear route to an eligibility review.

  1. Check your Premium Account.

    Read the selected account’s fee, virtual capital, target and refund conditions before subscribing. Eligibility is plan-specific.

  2. Achieve the eligible target.

    Meet the account’s target while satisfying all applicable trading and evaluation rules. Reaching a displayed target alone does not establish refund approval.

  3. Ask for an eligibility review.

    Use in-app support to confirm the request procedure. Keep your account details, payment reference and target-completion record ready; never send your password.

  4. Receive the review outcome.

    The applicable plan terms determine the refundable amount, exclusions, payment method and processing time. Confirm these with support before paying.
