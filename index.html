The problem is real

Practice before the stakes get real.

93%

of individual equity F&O traders incurred losses during FY22–FY24.

SEBI study · September 2024

The real challenge goes beyond finding an entry.

SEBI reported aggregate individual-trader losses above ₹1.8 lakh crore over those three years. Its July 2025 release also reported that nearly 91% of individual equity-derivatives traders lost money in FY2025.

Overtrading, impulsive position sizing and chasing losses are behaviours worth examining in your own practice. These figures describe market outcomes; they do not establish that emotions caused every loss or that a simulator can prevent losses.

Trading psychology

Train the pause. Not just the trade.

Before the trade: write the plan

Define your setup, exit and maximum acceptable risk before entering. If the setup is absent, practise sitting out.

During the trade: pause the impulse

When fear, excitement or the urge to recover a loss appears, step away. Avoid changing your plan just to feel better.

After the trade: review the process

Record what you intended, what you did, and what you felt. Compare sessions and choose one habit to work on next.

What better practice can look like.

Illustrative learning goals, not customer testimonials.

“I want to stop chasing losses.”

A useful practice goal: pause after a losing trade and only return when the next setup meets your written rules.

“I need to understand my patterns.”

A useful practice goal: review your trading activity weekly and identify when you depart from your plan.

“I want consistency, not a lucky day.”

A useful practice goal: repeat a measured routine and assess decisions across multiple sessions.
