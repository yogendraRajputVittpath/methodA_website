Why your practice needs a stopping rule

Decide how a session ends before emotions start making the decision.

MethodA Editorial · 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Define your practice boundaries

Choose a session limit and a consistent approach to position sizing before you begin. Premium Account rules may impose additional requirements; read those first.

Separate a loss from a new opportunity

A losing trade does not make the next trade more likely to succeed. Evaluate the next setup independently and avoid increasing size simply to recover the previous loss.

Keep the review measurable

Compare your actions across several sessions. Look for repeated rule changes, rushed decisions or inconsistent sizing. Virtual performance does not establish how you will perform with real capital.
