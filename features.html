Build emotional discipline

Turn reactions into a routine.

Before the trade: write the plan

Define your setup, exit and maximum acceptable risk before entering. If the setup is absent, practise sitting out.

During the trade: pause the impulse

When fear, excitement or the urge to recover a loss appears, step away. Avoid changing your plan just to feel better.

After the trade: review the process

Record what you intended, what you did, and what you felt. Compare sessions and choose one habit to work on next.
