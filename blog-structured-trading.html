Trading Discipline

A trading routine you can actually repeat

Build structure before, during and after your simulated trading session.

MethodA Editorial · 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Start before the first order

Write the conditions that make a trade valid for your method. Include an exit condition and a limit for the session. Treat these as practice rules, rather than promises about market direction.

Use a deliberate pause

Before entering, compare the trade with your written plan. If you are responding mainly to excitement, frustration or fear of missing out, pause and reassess.

Review the process

After the session, compare your intended actions with what you actually did. Pick one specific behaviour to work on next time. Simulated profit is only one part of the review.
