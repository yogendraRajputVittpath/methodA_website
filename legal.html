Disclaimer

Important information about MethodA's simulated environment and financial-market positioning.

Current disclosure:

1. Simulated trading only

MethodA is a software platform for simulated trading, skill evaluation and self-improvement. No real-money trading is conducted through the platform.

2. No brokerage or investment service

MethodA does not execute real trades, hold securities, manage client funds or portfolios, or act as a stock broker, investment adviser, research analyst or portfolio manager.

3. No recommendations or guaranteed outcomes

Platform analytics, scores and educational content are not investment recommendations or guarantees of future trading performance. Historical or simulated results do not guarantee future results.

4. User responsibility

Any decision to trade in live markets is made independently by the user. Users should consider their own circumstances and consult an appropriately registered professional for personalized financial advice.

5. Market risk

Real-world securities and derivatives involve market risk. Simulation cannot fully reproduce the financial, psychological, liquidity and execution conditions of live trading.
