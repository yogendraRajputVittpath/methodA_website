MethodA FAQ

The details that matter.

Is MethodA a brokerage?

No. MethodA provides simulated trading and tools for learning and self-review. It does not execute real-market trades or manage a trading portfolio for you.

What markets can I practise?

MethodA is designed for Indian stocks and F&O simulation. Check current instruments and data availability in the application.

Does virtual trading mean everything is free?

No. Trading balances are virtual, but Premium Account subscription fees are real payments. Review current prices in the app.

How does MethodA help with emotional trading?

Use a written plan, a deliberate pause when emotions rise, and a review of your trading activity. These habits can help you identify impulsive decisions; the platform does not guarantee emotional control or profitability.

Can I get a refund after achieving my target?

Refund eligibility follows your selected Premium Account terms. Target completion must satisfy the applicable rules. Ask in-app support to confirm the amount, exclusions, request procedure and processing time. Simulated profits are not withdrawable money.

How do I use coins in the Store?

Open MethodA Store, check your eligible coin or credit balance, select an available item, and review its redemption terms before confirming. Prices and availability may change.

How many traders use MethodA?

MethodA reports 3,000+ registered traders and 2,000+ traders who improved their trading patterns as of October 2026. These company-reported figures refer to trading patterns, not verified profitability.

Is MethodA registered with SEBI?

MethodA states that it is not a SEBI-registered entity. It provides simulated trading software, not personalised investment advice. References to SEBI studies do not imply an endorsement.

Can simulated performance predict live results?

No. Real markets involve financial pressure, execution conditions, costs and risks that simulation may not fully reproduce.
