# Content review and source notes

Reviewed 5 October 2026.

## User-supplied facts

The owner supplied 3,000+ registered traders and 2,000+ traders who improved their trading patterns. The site labels these company-reported figures. No assessment methodology, time period or independent verification was provided; do not convert the figures into a success rate or profit claim.

Refund rules were described by the owner as being as per Premium Accounts. The website consequently explains plan-specific eligibility and how to seek confirmation; it does not invent targets, a refund percentage, a deadline, an automatic approval promise or a processing duration. Confirm exact plan terms before adding more specific promotional claims.

No authentic customer quotes were supplied. The trader-perspective section contains clearly labelled illustrative learning goals, not testimonials or fictitious customer identities. Replace with approved quotes only when available.

## Application review

Confirmed visible routes and content:

- https://app.methoda.in/pages/home — guest dashboard, watchlist, virtual funds, closed P&L, profit/loss/breakeven counts, indices, charts and top movers.
- https://app.methoda.in/auth/Login — current login form.
- https://app.methoda.in/auth/Register — current registration form.
- https://app.methoda.in/pages/Subscription — Beginner Trader, Intermediate Trader and Expert Trader; custom-plan option; virtual capital and target; listings for journal, analytics, tools and reports.
- https://app.methoda.in/pages/MethodAStore — store, credits, product listings and purchase history entry point. Headphones were visible. No purchase was made.
- https://app.methoda.in/pages/TradeAnalytics — analytics date/exchange/type filters; data remained loading during review.

The old https://app.methoda.in/login returns Unmatched Route. The supplied credentials were entered on the current login page. Keyboard activation of the login control confirmed a validation error: the password must be at least eight characters long, while the supplied password contains six. A successful authenticated session was therefore not established. No trades, subscriptions, redemptions or account changes were performed. Review was based on accessible app screens and public product descriptions; private account workflows remain unverified.

The app displayed repeated example-like prices/capital/targets across its Premium Account cards. The new marketing site links to current plans rather than presenting those values as settled commercial terms.

Detailed claims from the original redesign that were not confirmed in the app (e.g. options Greeks and behavioural flags) were not carried forward as verified capabilities.
